Child Safety & CSAE Standards

App Name: The Red Thread (Android: com.theredthread.app, also available on the App Store)

Zero Tolerance Policy: The Red Thread has a zero-tolerance policy regarding the sexual abuse and exploitation of children (CSAE). We strictly prohibit Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and any content or behaviour that endangers minors. Any account involved is permanently banned.

The Red Thread is an app for sharing activities, photo challenges and memories with friends. These standards apply to everything shared in The Red Thread: photos, quest proofs, memories, profiles, names, captions and group content.

1. Prohibited Content and Conduct

We strictly prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation in any form, including, without limitation:

  • child sexual abuse material (CSAM), including drawn, digitally altered or AI-generated imagery;
  • any sexualised content involving, or appearing to involve, a minor;
  • grooming, sexual solicitation of a minor, or attempts to obtain sexual content from a minor;
  • sextortion, and any threat to share intimate images;
  • trafficking or any other exploitation of children;
  • sharing, requesting or promoting any of the above, including links to such content outside the app.

2. Who Can See What You Share

The Red Thread is not a public social network. There is no public feed, no discovery of strangers and no private messaging. Photos and proofs are shared only with friends you have accepted, or with the members of a thread or challenge group you have joined by invitation.

3. Reporting Inside the App

Without leaving the app, users can:

  • Report a proof photo: open the photo, tap ⋯, choose Report this proof, then select a reason (for example Sexual content).
  • Block a user: from a friend's profile, or from the ⋯ menu of any of their photos. Blocking removes the friendship and hides each person's content from the other.

Each report is recorded with the reporter, the reported account, the photo concerned and the reason. When an account receives reports from several different people who share a thread or a group with it, it is immediately prevented from posting and placed in a review queue.

4. Reporting by Email

You can also contact our child safety point of contact directly (see section 6), including if you do not use the app. Please include the name or username of the account concerned and a description of what you saw. Do not send us copies of illegal images.

5. What We Do

When content that may involve the abuse or exploitation of a child is reported to us, we:

  1. review it as a priority, as soon as possible;
  2. remove the content and permanently ban the account(s) involved;
  3. report Child Sexual Abuse Material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to the competent national authorities (in France: PHAROS, internet-signalement.gouv.fr), as required by law;
  4. preserve the relevant data as required by law, and cooperate with law enforcement requests.

We comply with all applicable child safety laws and regulations, including the reporting obligations that apply to online services.

This contact can speak to our CSAM prevention practices and compliance, and handles child safety reports in priority.
